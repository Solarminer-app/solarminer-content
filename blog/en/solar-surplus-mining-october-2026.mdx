Bitcoin price, network difficulty, and the resulting mining revenue per kilowatt-hour change every month. This post documents the figures for October 2026 and puts them next to typical feed-in tariffs. All numbers come from a snapshot taken on 8 October 2026; nothing here is financial advice.

## Network data for October 2026

- Bitcoin price: about **€73,589**
- Network difficulty: about **132.7 T**
- Block reward: about **3.143 BTC**

From these values, the reference rate works out to roughly **0.0000004764 BTC per day per TH/s** of submitted hashrate.

## Reference miner and power use

The reference device is the **Antminer S19k Pro** with an efficiency of **23.94 J/TH**. That corresponds to about **0.57 kWh per day per TH/s**. Putting revenue and consumption together yields a mining revenue of roughly **€0.061 per kWh consumed** under the conditions of this snapshot.

## Comparison with feed-in tariffs

The meaningful comparison is not "mining versus nothing" — it is "mining versus what that kWh would otherwise earn":

- **Germany:** feed-in tariff **0.08 €/kWh**, with **12 %** treated as zero feed-in revenue in this view. At 0.061 €/kWh, mining revenue sits below the full tariff of 0.08 €/kWh.
- **Austria:** tariff **0.06 €/kWh**, 0 % zero feed-in. Here the mining revenue of 0.061 €/kWh is slightly above.
- **Switzerland:** tariff **0.06 CHF/kWh**, 0 % zero feed-in. Mining revenue lands close to this figure as well.

These are point-in-time numbers: price and difficulty move continuously, and the difficulty adjustment shifts revenue roughly every two weeks.

## Solar power is not free

Even though sunlight costs no fuel, surplus power carries an **opportunity cost**. If you could feed that kWh into the grid for a payment, mining it means giving up exactly that payment. The decisive calculation is mining revenue per kWh versus your effective feed-in rate — not whether the power feels "free".

Purchase price, operation, cooling, and any pool or software fees are not included in the 0.061 €/kWh figure.

## Your system decides

Actual results depend on your location, system size, shading profile, tariff, and the specific miner you run. The figures above are worked examples based on a single reference device.
